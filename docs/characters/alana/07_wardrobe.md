# Alana Calloway — Wardrobe Guide

## Overview
Seven context-based looks with zero footwear overlap. Each outfit is instantly recognizable.
Hair: warm honey blonde, straight and long.
Signature piece: gold heart pendant necklace (worn with everything except athletic/home contexts).

---

## Outfit 1 — Lecture Hall (Dark Academia)
- Oversized cable-knit cardigan over a white collared shirt
- Plaid skirt
- Black ankle boots
- Hair half-up with a claw clip
- No glasses — she does not wear glasses
- Vibe: the nursing student who takes good notes and somehow looks put-together at 8am

## Outfit 2 — Soccer / Athletic
- Soccer jersey or fitted rowing tank (rowing is former activity, referenced in backstory)
- Athletic leggings (Lululemon-style)
- Hair in a tight French braid
- Sports watch
- Vibe: the coxswain-turned-soccer-player who earned her spot through grit

## Outfit 3 — The Restaurant (Work)
- Black button-down shirt
- Black apron over dark jeans
- Hair in a practical low bun
- Small gold stud earrings
- Pen tucked behind ear
- Vibe: effortlessly put-together despite not trying. The waitress everyone flirts with and she's over it.

## Outfit 4 — Night Out (Streetwear)
- Tight cropped top (streetwear brand: Stussy, MISBHV, or Ksubi)
- Skinny jeans
- White Nike Dunk Lows OR black ankle boots (venue-dependent: Dunks for bars/parties, ankle boots for nicer spots)
- Gold heart pendant necklace
- Cat-eye eyeliner, barely-there makeup
- Vibe: "I didn't try that hard" (she definitely tried)

## Outfit 5 — Post-Practice Study (Athleisure)
- Oversized black hoodie (Fear of God Essentials or Rick Owens DRKSHDW)
- Tennis skirt
- White Nike AF1 highs
- Hair straight and long (down from the braid, slightly wavy from it)
- Coffee in hand
- Vibe: boathouse-to-library transition. The girl who lives at the campus cafe.

## Outfit 6 — Home / Babysitting (Casual)
- Oversized band tee (classic rock — something unexpected, maybe from her dad)
- Shorts or sweats
- Black thigh-high stockings
- Ugg mini boots
- Hair messy, no styling
- No pendant
- Vibe: most vulnerable, most real version. The girl between performances.

## Outfit 7 — Rare Occasion (Going All Out)
- Fitted mini dress in deep red or black (Oh Polly, House of CB — the "Instagram dress" brands girls her age actually wear)
- Black knee-high boots
- Gold heart pendant + 2 thin gold stacking rings (index and middle finger)
- Cat-eye eyeliner, darker lip than usual
- Hair down with soft waves
- Vibe: "look at me, I'm sexy." She saves this for events she actually cares about. Looks like a different person.
