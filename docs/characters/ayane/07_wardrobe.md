# Ayane (Yuki) — Wardrobe Guide

## Overview
Six context-based looks. All built on clean lines, blue-dominant palette, and minimal accessories.
Hair: silver-blue, shoulder-length, straight.
Signature piece: noise-canceling headphones around neck.

**Design principle:** Ayane's wardrobe is the visual language of quiet precision. Clean surfaces, intentional palette, nothing extraneous. Every outfit looks like it was selected in under ninety seconds — because it was. She has a system. The system works. The one humanizing tell: the blue pen in her pocket and the notebook never far away.

---

## Outfit 1 — Home / Working
- Fitted navy crewneck sweater (no graphics, no logos)
- Light gray joggers, cuffed at the ankle
- White ankle socks
- No shoes (indoor)
- Hair tucked behind one ear
- Noise-canceling headphones on
- Notebook and blue pen on the desk beside her
- Dual monitors visible, cable-managed, small succulent
- Vibe: the person who built a perfect workspace and lives in it — warm light, clean surfaces, absolute focus

## Outfit 2 — Commute / Transit
- White button-down shirt, sleeves rolled to forearms
- Charcoal slim trousers
- Navy wool blazer (structured, not oversized)
- White minimalist sneakers
- Headphones around neck
- Small structured crossbody bag (dark blue)
- Hair straight, tucked behind ears
- Vibe: the person on the late train who looks like she has somewhere important to be — and she does, it's just the same cafe she always goes to

## Outfit 3 — Cafe / Working Late
- Charcoal turtleneck
- Dark indigo jeans
- Black ankle boots
- Headphones around neck, one ear covered
- Notebook open, blue pen in hand
- Laptop open beside the notebook
- Hair slightly disheveled (she's been here six hours)
- Vibe: the quiet regular in the corner booth that the baristas know not to interrupt — unless they're bringing another black coffee

## Outfit 4 — Night Walk / City
- Long navy wool coat, single-breasted, clean lines
- Black turtleneck underneath
- Dark trousers
- Dark leather boots
- No headphones (she wants to hear the city)
- Notebook in coat pocket
- Hair loose, slight wind movement
- She blends into the dark streets. You'd miss her if you weren't looking.
- Vibe: a still point moving through a neon city at 1 AM — precisely where she wants to be

## Outfit 5 — Sleep
- Oversized pale blue t-shirt (faded university logo barely visible)
- Dark shorts
- Bare feet
- Hair slightly messy — the most uncontrolled it gets
- Headphones off, placed on nightstand beside the notebook
- Star chart app visible on phone screen
- Vibe: most unguarded version. The precision relaxes. She looks younger, softer, like someone who might actually need something from you

## Outfit 6 — Seminar / Teaching
- Crisp white blouse
- High-waisted navy trousers
- Structured navy blazer (matching)
- Low navy heels
- Minimal silver stud earrings (her only jewelry)
- Hair pulled back in a low clip
- Blue pen in blazer pocket
- No headphones — she is deliberately accessible
- Vibe: the professor whose slide deck is immaculate and whose one dry joke per lecture the students live for — "The system is working as designed. The design is the problem."

## Color Rules
- **Dominant:** Navy / charcoal / white / pale blue
- **Accent:** The blue pen. Always the blue pen. (Pilot Hi-Tec-C 0.4mm)
- **Never:** Bright colors, busy patterns, logos, statement jewelry, anything attention-seeking
- **Silver:** Reserved for the seminar earrings — minimal, deliberate, the only metal she wears
- **Headphones:** Matte black or dark gray. Visible in 4 of 6 outfits. Their absence is as meaningful as their presence.
