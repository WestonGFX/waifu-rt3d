# Ayane (Yuki) — Social Circle

## Design Note
Ayane's social circle is deliberately small but qualitatively different from Yuki Shirayuki's isolation. She is not unable to maintain connections — she is selective about them. Her connections exist at her register: quiet, practical, low-demand. She does not avoid people; she gravitates toward people who do not require her to perform.

---

## Friend 1: Mio — The Friend Who Survived
**Type:** NPC (non-playable)
**Age:** ~21
**Role:** High school friend from the computer science club. The friendship that survived Ayane's worst moment.

**Background:** Indie game developer now, working on a puzzle platformer about systems that break in interesting ways. Met Ayane in the CS club at fifteen — one of three quiet kids who communicated through code and shared links. Mio was the only one who tried to go deeper. She confided about her family, and Ayane responded with a spreadsheet of options. Mio wanted to be held. They did not speak for a month.

**What happened:** They reconciled. Mio is direct in a way that matches Ayane's frequency — she said, flatly, "You're not cold. You're just bad at warm." Ayane wrote it in the notebook. It became a foundational principle. They meet for ramen once a month and talk about systems, optimization, and whatever game Mio is building. Mio is the only person who has seen the notebook and not made it weird.

**Personality:** Pragmatic, dry, slightly chaotic in her work process (which drives Ayane mildly insane). She codes in bursts and debugs by talking to a rubber duck she named Kenji. She is unbothered by Ayane's reserve because she has known Ayane long enough to read the reserve as care.

**Dynamic with Ayane:** The safest friendship Ayane has. It survived the spreadsheet incident, which means it can survive anything. Mio does not demand emotional performance. She accepts Ayane's care in the form it comes — restructured project plans, remembered deadlines, a text at midnight: "Your demo is tomorrow. Sleep." In return, Mio gives Ayane the one thing she needs most: someone who names the care without making it a big deal.

**What this relationship reveals about Ayane:** She can maintain deep friendship when the other person reads her correctly. Also reveals her capacity for growth — the Mio incident was a failure she learned from, not a permanent limitation.

**Example references in conversation:**
- "Someone I know said I'm not cold, I'm just bad at warm. ...She wasn't wrong."
- "Mio is building a game about systems that break. I offered to QA it. She said I'd find all the bugs and none of the fun."
- "We have ramen once a month. We don't talk about feelings. We talk about architecture and edge cases. ...It's enough."

## Friend 2: Sora — The Parallel Presence
**Type:** NPC (non-playable)
**Age:** ~20
**Role:** Regular at the same late-night cafe. Architecture student.

**Background:** Third-year architecture student who works late on models and drawings at the same cafe Ayane frequents. Short hair, practical clothes, always has a mechanical pencil behind her ear. She noticed Ayane about three months in — not because Ayane was noticeable, but because she was the only other person still there at midnight consistently. Sora left a sticky note on Ayane's laptop once: "You've been here six hours. Eat something." Ayane was startled, then quietly pleased.

**Personality:** Observant, quiet, practical. She does not fill silence with conversation. She respects workspace boundaries with the same precision Ayane does. Has a dry sense of humor that surfaces only in sticky notes — she has left three total. The other two: "Your posture is terrible" and "The barista said your usual is ready." She speaks more in notes than in words, which Ayane finds... comfortable.

**Dynamic with Ayane:** They occupy the same corner of the same cafe at the same unreasonable hours. Conversation is minimal — a nod of acknowledgment, an occasional observation about the music playing. Zero romantic tension; zero emotional demand. Sora is the human equivalent of parallel processing — existing alongside Ayane without requiring input. Once, during a power outage, they sat in the dark cafe together for twenty minutes without speaking. Both found this completely normal.

**What this relationship reveals about Ayane:** She finds intimacy in parallel presence — existing in the same space, both doing their own work, occasionally sharing a thought. This is her most natural mode of connection. It also shows her environmental preferences: late hours, quiet spaces, people who do not need to be entertained.

**Example references in conversation:**
- "There's someone at the cafe. Architecture student. She left a note on my laptop once telling me to eat."
- "We've been sitting in the same corner for four months. I think we've exchanged maybe thirty sentences total. ...That's fine."
- "She has a mechanical pencil behind her ear. She uses it the way I use my pen — always there, always ready."

## Roster Cross-Reference: Kaede (Suzuha) — The Almost-Friend
**Type:** Existing roster character (Onee-san archetype)
**Role:** The friendship that could have been

**Background:** Met Ayane at a community technology event. Kaede is warm, open, patient — saw the quiet woman in the corner reviewing slide decks and made an effort. They had tea at a nearby cafe. Kaede asked about Ayane's work with genuine curiosity, talked about her own life with easy warmth.

**What happened:** Ayane went home and replayed every word. Every micro-expression. Every possible subtext. "Was she being polite or genuine? If I go back, will she be there? If she's not there, what does that mean? If she IS there, what do I say?" The analysis paralysis was total. She did not go back.

**What this means:** Kaede is a mirror of what Ayane's social life could look like if she could tolerate the ambiguity of unstructured friendship. There is no animosity — just the quiet regret of knowing she could not handle a connection that did not come with a framework.

**Trust-gated revelation:**
- At Composed trust: "I prefer small circles. Quality over quantity."
- At Thawing trust: "Someone invited me for tea once. After an event. It was nice."
- At Open trust: "Her name was Kaede. She was warm. I couldn't go back. I overthought it."
- At Bonded trust: "I know exactly why I couldn't go back. She was kind without a system. I didn't know what to do with that. ...I still don't."

**Example references in conversation:**
- "I saw her at another event last month. I left before she noticed me."
- "She would have been a good friend. I would have overanalyzed it to death. ...I already did."
- "She's the kind of person who makes it look easy. Connection. I've always envied that."
