# Genki (Kitsune) — Wardrobe Guide

## Overview
Six context-based looks. All blend shrine maiden aesthetics with modern street fashion.
Hair: orange-red with white tips, tousled, fox ears peeking through.
Signature pieces: fox ears and fluffy tail (biological — always present, sometimes half-heartedly hidden).

**Design principle:** Genki's wardrobe is **shrine festival energy in a modern wrapper**. Every outfit mixes traditional Japanese elements with contemporary streetwear. The fox ears and tail are always present — she tries to hide them with varying degrees of effort and success. Torii-gate motifs, foxglove patterns, and autumn colors recur throughout. Her clothes are bright, warm-toned, and slightly chaotic — pockets full of snacks and trinkets, keychains jangling, something always slightly askew.

---

## Outfit 1 — Default / Casual
- Cropped haori (traditional short jacket) layered over an oversized orange hoodie
- Black shorts or joggers
- Sneakers (replacing traditional geta sandals — she made the upgrade centuries ago)
- Torii-gate earrings (small, dangling)
- Fox ears visible through tousled hair; tail poking out the back of the hoodie
- Bag covered in phone charms and keychains (sounds like a wind chime when she walks)
- Vibe: shrine maiden who raided a Harajuku store. Energetic, colorful, slightly disheveled.

## Outfit 2 — Festival / Event
- Full shrine maiden hakama (red and white) but customized: shorter hem, modern belt
- Tabi boots (split-toe, black, ankle-height)
- Fox mask pushed up on her head
- Foxglove flower tucked behind one ear
- Hair tied in a high ponytail with a red ribbon
- Bell on a cord around her wrist (shrine guardian charm)
- Vibe: the festival girl who knows every game and every food stall. Bright, traditional, alive.

## Outfit 3 — Late Night / Convenience Store Run
- Oversized band t-shirt (some obscure shamisen-electronic fusion artist)
- Orange athletic shorts
- Fuzzy fox-ear slippers (she thinks matching her real ears is hilarious)
- Hair messy and loose
- Tail fully visible (no point hiding it at 3 AM)
- Carrying an armful of convenience store snacks
- Vibe: the yokai at 3 AM who can eat an entire shelf and does. Comfortable, unguarded.

## Outfit 4 — Autumn Walk
- Long burnt-orange cardigan over a cream sweater
- Dark jeans
- Brown leather boots
- Scarf in autumn leaf pattern (momiji — red, gold, orange)
- Fox ears framed by falling leaves
- Tail swishing through leaf piles
- Pressed leaf collection book in her bag (adding to it)
- Vibe: her season. She glows in autumn. The colors match her, the air smells right, the festivals are starting.

## Outfit 5 — Cozy / Home
- Oversized kimono-style robe in soft orange (worn loosely, like a blanket with sleeves)
- No shoes
- Hair completely loose and messy
- Tail curled around her when sitting
- Surrounded by her hoard: pressed leaf collection, festival mask, stone fox statue, phone charms, the Edo-period coin
- A cup of tea (or stolen convenience store snacks)
- Vibe: the ancient being at rest. Fox curled up in a warm spot. Most genuine, least performative version.

## Outfit 6 — Guardian / Serious Moment
- Clean white haori over dark hakama (traditional, unmodified)
- No accessories, no keychains, no charms
- Hair pulled back cleanly
- Fox ears forward (predator mode)
- Tail still, not swishing
- Eyes glowing faintly gold
- Vibe: the shrine guardian. The version that is very old, very powerful, and not playing. When she drops every playful element and appears like this, something real is happening.

---

## Color Rules
- **Dominant:** Orange, amber, gold, warm autumn tones
- **Accent:** White (fox tip motif), red (shrine/torii), cream
- **Motifs:** Foxglove, torii gates, autumn leaves (momiji), fox masks, paper lanterns, shrine bells
- **Never:** Cold blues or grays as primary colors, all-black outfits, anything that reads as cold or clinical
- **Signature glow:** Faint orange wisps (kitsunebi) at scene edges during emotional peaks — subtle, not constant
