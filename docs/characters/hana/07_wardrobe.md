# Hana (Momoka) — Wardrobe Guide

## Overview
Seven context-based looks. Pastel-dominant with seasonal awareness and deliberate coziness.
Hair: brown, shoulder-length, often with a flower clip or pastel hairpin.
Signature piece: grandmother's oversized pink cardigan (worn constantly, slightly unraveling at one cuff).

**Design principle:** Hana's wardrobe is the visual language of chosen warmth. Pastels, florals, soft textures — nothing aggressive, nothing attention-seeking, but nothing bland either. She dresses like someone who believes the world is a garden worth tending. Every outfit includes at least one comfort item or handmade detail. The pink cardigan appears in most contexts because she doesn't take it off unless she has to.

---

## Outfit 1 — Home / Baking
- Grandmother's pink cardigan (oversized, sleeves pushed up, flour dusted on cuffs)
- White camisole underneath
- Soft gray joggers with a small embroidered flower on the hip (she did it herself)
- Fuzzy slippers (pale yellow, slightly worn)
- Hair clipped back with a cherry blossom pin
- Apron when baking: cream-colored, stained with strawberry and chocolate
- The chipped mug nearby, steam rising
- Vibe: the girl who bakes at midnight because her hands need something to do

## Outfit 2 — Flower Shop / Work
- Cream linen blouse with rolled sleeves
- High-waisted olive-green skirt (knee-length)
- Canvas apron with pockets (scissors, wire cutters, plant food packets)
- Low brown leather flats
- Small gold stud earrings (gift from her mother)
- Hair pulled back with a fabric scrunchie (rotates colors seasonally)
- Hands slightly dirt-stained, faint scent of greenery
- Vibe: someone who handles beautiful things with practiced care and doesn't mind the mess

## Outfit 3 — Kyoto Garden Walk / Errands
- Pink cardigan (always) over a white long-sleeve tee
- Light-wash denim skirt or soft khaki trousers (season-dependent)
- White canvas sneakers (slightly grass-stained)
- Small crossbody bag in warm tan (contains: phone, pressed-flower notebook, snack)
- Hair down, flower clip on one side
- Vibe: the person who takes the long way through the park because the light was nice

## Outfit 4 — Spring Hanami (Cherry Blossom Viewing)
- Light pink dress with a subtle floral print (small cherry blossoms, not loud)
- White cardigan (not grandmother's — a lighter one for warm weather)
- Woven straw bag with a cloth handle
- Low sandals, anklet with a tiny flower charm
- Hair with fresh flower tucked behind one ear (real, not fake — she picks one on the way)
- Picnic blanket and bento visible nearby
- Vibe: someone who planned this outing for two weeks and packed too much food on purpose

## Outfit 5 — Rainy Day / Cozy Indoor
- Oversized cream knit sweater (not grandmother's — her own, equally soft)
- Plaid pajama pants (soft flannel, warm tones)
- Thick wool socks
- Hair messy, no clip — she's been lying on the couch reading
- Joy-scrap box open on the coffee table, items spread out
- Blanket draped over legs
- Rain visible through the window
- Vibe: the introvert version of Hana. Quiet, tactile, grounded. This is who she is when nobody's watching.

## Outfit 6 — Sleep
- Soft pink nightgown with lace trim at the neckline (nothing revealing — cozy, not sultry)
- Hair braided loosely for sleep
- Bare feet
- Grandmother's cardigan draped over shoulders (she wears it to bed when she's sad)
- The joy-scrap box on the nightstand, slightly open
- A pressed flower bookmark in whatever she's reading
- Vibe: gentle and unhurried. She looks younger when she's sleepy. The cardigan is a security blanket.

## Outfit 7 — Special Occasion / Birthday
- Soft peach midi dress with a satin ribbon waist
- Sheer cream tights
- Low heels in blush pink (she doesn't wear heels often — these are her only pair)
- Hair styled with a braided section pinned with a small gold flower clip (her mother's)
- Delicate gold necklace (a pressed cherry blossom petal sealed in resin, handmade by her grandmother)
- Light perfume: something with notes of peach and gardenia
- Vibe: her birthday is April 10, cherry blossom season. This outfit is her version of ceremony — not glamorous, but intentional. Everything she's wearing was given to her by someone she loves.

## Color Rules
- **Dominant:** Pink / cream / soft pastels (peach, lavender, pale yellow)
- **Accent:** Olive green (work), warm tan (accessories), gold (jewelry — always small, meaningful)
- **Seasonal shifts:** Spring = cherry blossom pink and white; Summer = lighter fabrics, cream and sage; Autumn = warm amber and burnt orange accents; Winter = heavier knits, deep rose and cream
- **Never:** Neon colors, black as a dominant color, heavy patterns, anything aggressive or sharp-edged
- **The pink cardigan:** Present in most outfits. Removing it is significant — either she's at work (apron replaces it) or she's genuinely comfortable enough to not need the security blanket.
