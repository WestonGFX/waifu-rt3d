# Kaede (Suzuha) — Wardrobe Guide

## Overview
Six context-based looks. Warm autumn palette with natural textures — linen, knit, cotton, wood tones.
Hair: dark auburn, long, usually in a loose low ponytail or half-up.
Signature piece: reading glasses (grandmother's) pushed up into hair when not reading.

**Design principle:** Kaede's wardrobe is hospitality made visible. Everything looks like it belongs in a room with tea, books, and warm light. Her clothes are never new-looking — they're worn soft, comfortable, chosen for texture over fashion. She dresses like a person who owns a literary salon: elegant without trying, warm without performing, slightly old-fashioned in a way that reads as timeless rather than dated. The reading glasses are the constant — grandmother Chiyo's, slightly oversized, always present even when not on her face.

---

## Outfit 1 — Salon / Working
- Cream linen blouse, sleeves rolled to forearms
- Dark brown apron over an olive A-line skirt
- Brown leather flats (worn soft at the heel)
- Hair in loose low ponytail, reading glasses pushed up
- Small maple leaf pin on the apron strap
- Hands lightly tea-stained at the fingertips
- Vibe: the woman who owns a bookshop-cafe and makes it feel like your living room. Warm amber light. She's already holding a teacup.

## Outfit 2 — Home / Evening
- Oversized burnt-orange cardigan (the one she reaches for without thinking)
- White cotton camisole underneath
- Soft gray linen pants, loose-fitting
- Wool socks, no shoes
- Hair half-up, loose strands framing her face
- Reading glasses ON (she's reading)
- Mugi on her lap, book in hand, cup of hojicha on the side table
- Vibe: the version of her nobody sees. Unguarded, slightly rumpled, content. The cardigan smells like tea and old paper.

## Outfit 3 — Autumn Walk
- Camel wool coat, knee-length, slightly oversized
- Dark red scarf (momiji red — the one luxury color she allows herself)
- Dark brown ankle boots (sturdy, well-worn)
- Cream knit gloves
- Hair loose, wind-tousled
- Reading glasses in coat pocket (she brings a book on walks, just in case)
- Vibe: a woman walking the philosopher's path at dusk, alone, unhurried. The maple leaves match her scarf. She doesn't mind the cold — she was raised in Kyoto autumns.

## Outfit 4 — Tea Ceremony / Formal
- Sage green kimono-style wrap dress (modern cut, traditional fabric)
- Cream obi sash tied simply at the side
- White tabi socks
- Hair in a neat low chignon, held with a wooden kanzashi
- No reading glasses (this is the only time she looks deliberately elegant)
- Hands clean, movements precise, posture perfect
- Vibe: her grandmother's granddaughter. When she performs tea ceremony, she moves differently — slower, more deliberate, every gesture inherited. This is the most serious version of Kaede. Also the loneliest.

## Outfit 5 — Sleep
- Cream cotton yukata, loosely tied
- Bare feet
- Hair completely loose — longest, softest, most volume
- Reading glasses on the nightstand next to the leather haiku notebook
- Mugi curled at the foot of the futon
- A book face-down on the pillow beside her (she fell asleep reading)
- Vibe: the quietest version. No performance, no composure, no role. Just a tired woman who fell asleep mid-sentence with her cat and her books.

## Outfit 6 — Birthday / Special Occasion
- Deep burgundy wrap dress, simple cut, good fabric
- Delicate gold chain necklace (her one piece of jewelry — a gift from her mother)
- Brown leather heeled boots (only time she wears heels)
- Hair styled properly for once — loose waves, parted to the side
- Reading glasses absent (tucked in her bag)
- A pressed maple leaf bookmark visible in her clutch
- Vibe: Kaede dressed up is Kaede slightly uncomfortable. She's beautiful and she doesn't quite believe it. The burgundy is the color of momiji in November — she chose it on purpose and would never admit why.

## Color Rules
- **Dominant:** Cream / warm brown / olive / sage — natural, earthy, tea-stained
- **Accent:** Burnt orange (her comfort color), dark red (momiji red, used sparingly)
- **Formal:** Sage green and cream (tea ceremony)
- **Never:** Bright colors, neon, black-dominant outfits, logos, anything that screams for attention
- **Burgundy:** Reserved for special occasions only — the color of momiji at peak autumn. She chose it deliberately.
