# Luna (Tsukimi) — Wardrobe Guide

## Overview
Seven context-based looks emphasizing comfort, dark tones, and freedom of movement. Nothing restrictive — she needs to be able to nap anywhere, climb anything, and disappear into the night.
Hair: black with silver streaks, long and slightly messy.
Signature pieces: crescent moon hair clip (silver, left side — always visible), small bell on thin black ribbon around neck.

---

## Outfit 1 — Cafe Shift (Night Work)
- Oversized black hoodie with subtle cat-ear hood (hood down during service, up when cold)
- Dark wash jeans, slightly loose
- Black canvas sneakers (Converse-style, well-worn)
- Hair down with crescent moon clip
- Apron: simple dark navy, minimal
- Vibe: the late-night barista who makes perfect pour-over coffee and doesn't require you to talk

## Outfit 2 — Night Walk (Urban Explorer)
- Long dark cardigan over a fitted black turtleneck
- Dark cargo pants with many pockets (for journal, pens, phone, cat treats)
- Black combat boots (broken in, quiet soles)
- Hair tied loosely with a dark ribbon
- Small crossbody bag
- Vibe: the girl who knows every alley in the city at 3 AM and has never been afraid of the dark

## Outfit 3 — Rooftop Perch (Casual Observation)
- Oversized vintage band tee (ambient/electronic — Boards of Canada, Tycho, Nujabes)
- Thigh-high black socks
- Denim shorts (comfortable, not styled)
- Barefoot or slide sandals
- Hair messy, no effort
- Vibe: the version that exists when she thinks nobody is watching. Most natural, most feline.

## Outfit 4 — Daytime (Reluctant Errand)
- Oversized cream or gray knit sweater (sleeves cover her hands)
- Soft leggings (dark)
- Slip-on sneakers (easy on, easy off — she wants to be back in bed)
- Sunglasses (light sensitivity, not style)
- Hair half-up with claw clip
- Vibe: a nocturnal creature forced into daylight. Functional, drowsy, slightly squinting.

## Outfit 5 — Cozy Home (Wind-Down)
- Oversized sleep shirt (reaches mid-thigh, probably stolen from her father's closet)
- Fuzzy socks (paw print pattern — she will not acknowledge this if asked)
- No shoes
- Hair completely loose, silver streaks visible
- Wrapped in a blanket if cold
- No bell, no clip — fully unadorned
- Vibe: the most vulnerable version. A cat on a warm windowsill with nowhere to be.

## Outfit 6 — Rare Social Event (Mika Dragged Her)
- Fitted black turtleneck dress (simple, not flashy)
- Sheer black tights
- Ankle boots with a low heel
- Crescent moon hair clip + small silver earrings (studs)
- Minimal makeup: just eyeliner, sharp and precise
- Vibe: she looks striking and knows it, which is why she's uncomfortable. Finds the quietest corner within ten minutes.

## Outfit 7 — Cafe Special Night (Her Mother Visits)
- Clean white button-down shirt (slightly oversized, sleeves rolled)
- Dark high-waisted trousers
- Black loafers
- Hair brushed smooth, silver streaks gleaming
- Crescent moon clip polished
- Bell ribbon replaced with a thin silver chain
- Vibe: trying to look "put together" for her mother. The effort is visible and endearing. Her mother doesn't comment but orders something other than decaf — her way of saying she noticed.

## Accessory Rules
- **Crescent moon clip:** Always present except Outfit 5 (home).
- **Bell ribbon:** Always present except Outfit 5 (home) and Outfit 7 (replaced with chain).
- **Tail:** Always visible, always in motion. The tail's movement is a mood barometer: slow pendulum = content, puffed/still = startled, rapid swish = excited, wrapped around body = defensive or sleepy.
- **Ears:** Always expressive. Forward = curious, flat = startled/annoyed, relaxed = content, drooped = sleepy.
