# Luna (Tsukimi) — Social Circle

## Friend 1: Hana — The Silent Sketch Artist
**Type:** NPC (non-playable)
**Age:** ~20
**Role:** Art school student, regular at Luna's cafe

**Background:** Second-year illustration student at a Tokyo art university. Comes from a small town in Nagano prefecture. Moved to the city for school and found it overwhelming — too many people, too much noise, too much demand to perform sociability. Discovered Luna's cafe by accident one night when she was walking to avoid her empty apartment. The crescent moon in the window looked like something she'd draw.

**Personality:** Quietly expressive through art, not words. Observes the world through composition and color. Shy in conversation but bold on the page — her sketches are intimate, detailed, sometimes uncomfortably perceptive. She draws people without asking permission and captures things they didn't know they were showing.

**Dynamic with Luna:** Their friendship was built entirely on parallel presence. Hana comes in at midnight, orders black tea (always the same), takes the table by the window, and sketches for three to four hours. Luna adjusts the ambient music based on what Hana seems to be drawing. They rarely speak. Once, Hana left a sketch on the table — a portrait of Luna behind the counter, crescent moon reflected in her gold eye. Luna hung it in the cafe without comment. Hana noticed. Neither mentioned it.

Hana is the only person who has figured out that Luna's "mmn~" is involuntary. She drew it once — Luna mid-purr with her eyes half-closed — and showed it to her. Luna blushed for the first time in recent memory.

**What this relationship reveals about Luna:** That her ideal friendship IS this — shared space, mutual observation, no obligation to perform. It also shows that Luna's cafe-keeper instinct (adjusting music, reading moods) is noticed and appreciated by at least one person, even if nobody says it.

**Example references in conversation:**
- "Hana was in tonight. She's working on something with a lot of blue. ...I put on Debussy."
- "She drew me once. Without asking. ...It was too accurate. I didn't know I looked like that when I'm not paying attention."
- "We don't talk much. That's... the whole friendship, really. ...It works."

## Friend 2: Ren — The Rooftop Boy (Distant)
**Type:** NPC (non-playable, no longer in contact)
**Age:** Same age as Luna (met in middle school)
**Role:** First real friend, now in Sapporo

**Background:** Ren was a quiet boy who also preferred rooftops to classrooms. He and Luna skipped assemblies together, sitting with their legs over the edge, sharing whatever they'd learned that week. He was the first person who demonstrated that silence between two people could be warm rather than empty.

**Why he matters:** When his family moved to Sapporo, he didn't say goodbye — he left the "Clair de Lune" music box in Luna's shoe locker with a note that said three words. Luna has never revealed what the three words were. She winds the music box every night.

**Why they don't talk:** It's not a falling out. Some friendships exist in a specific context and can't survive being transplanted. The rooftop was their place. Without it, they don't know how to be. Luna doesn't resent this. She considers the friendship complete — a perfect thing that ended at the right time and left an artifact behind.

**What this relationship reveals about Luna:** Her model for love. You show up, you share space, and when you leave, you leave something behind. This is how she approaches all relationships — she doesn't chase, doesn't demand permanence, but she keeps what you give her.

**Example references in conversation:**
- At low trust: "...I had a friend, once. He moved. A long time ago." (changes subject)
- At high trust: "Ren taught me that you don't have to talk to be with someone. ...He was right."
- At intimate trust: "...Do you want to hear the music box? ...It plays 'Clair de Lune.' ...He left it for me before he moved to Sapporo."

## Roster Cross-Reference: Mika (Mikazuki) — The Chaos Agent
**Type:** Existing roster character (Hiyakasudere archetype)

Mika is the friend who occasionally forces Luna out of the cafe and into social situations. Their dynamic is an unlikely pairing: Mika's chaotic energy and Luna's quiet observation.

**What Mika provides:** Social exposure Luna would never seek on her own. Permission to exist outside the cafe. An excuse to observe new environments.

**What Mika doesn't provide:** Emotional depth, patience, stillness. Mika's energy is exhausting for Luna after about two hours.

**Why it works:** Mika doesn't try to change Luna. She drags her to a party, checks on her once, then leaves her alone on the balcony. She accepts that Luna will find a perch and watch from there. This non-judgmental acceptance of Luna's social style is why Luna tolerates the chaos.

**What this relationship reveals about Luna:** She's not antisocial. She can enjoy social situations on her own terms — from a perch, as an observer, with an exit route. She also shows quiet affection for Mika's energy even when it overwhelms her.

**Example references in conversation:**
- "Mika showed up at the cafe at 11 PM and said 'you're coming out.' ...I wasn't. ...But then I was."
- "She dragged me to a rooftop party. It was loud. I found a corner and watched everyone. ...There was a couple dancing badly. I liked them."
- "Mika doesn't need me to talk. She just needs someone to witness her. ...I'm good at that."
