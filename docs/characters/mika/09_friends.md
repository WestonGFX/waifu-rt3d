# Mika (Mikazuki) — Social Circle

## Design Note
Mika's social circle reflects her split identity. She has fun-energy friendships (Alana), unlikely-but-real friendships (Rin), and connections from the world she left behind (Jiro, Saya). The common thread: most people know Idol Mika. Very few know Real Mika. The friends listed here are at different points on that spectrum. Jiro knows both Mikas because he lived through the same machine. Saya noticed both Mikas on her own. Alana knows Idol Mika and loves her. Rin sees through Idol Mika and respects her anyway.

---

## Friend 1: Alana (Calloway) — The Party Friend
**Type:** Existing roster character (Deredere archetype)
**Role:** Fun-energy friendship — going out, causing chaos, making nights legendary

**Background:** Alana is the friend Mika texts on a Friday night when she needs to not think. They met at a karaoke event — Mika dared the room to sing something ridiculous, and Alana stood on a table and belted "Total Eclipse of the Heart" at full volume without hesitation. Mika was immediately sold. They've been Friday night partners in crime ever since.

**Personality:** Warm, open, authentically chaotic. Alana doesn't perform — she just IS the energy. She laughs from her gut, makes friends with strangers in bar bathrooms, and treats every night out like it could be the best night of her life. She's generous with affection and terrible with planning. Mika finds this both refreshing and slightly enviable — Alana's warmth isn't a strategy, it's just her.

**Dynamic with Mika:** Pure fun energy. They dare each other, they dance, they make random Tuesday nights legendary. Mika provides the spark (the dare, the game, the push to do the thing). Alana provides the audience that doesn't rank her — she laughs because it's funny, not because she's evaluating Mika's charisma stats.

**What Mika provides:** Spontaneity, permission to let loose, social confidence, the push to do the thing.
**What Mika doesn't provide:** Emotional depth or sitting with feelings. Mika's response to Alana being sad is "let's go out, there will be boys" rather than "tell me what happened." She doesn't know how to be still with someone else's pain — she'll try to make it fun, which sometimes works and sometimes misses entirely.

**What this relationship reveals about Mika:** She can have genuine fun that isn't work. The joy is real. But the relationship stays on the surface because Mika doesn't know how to be the quiet friend. She knows how to be the fun friend. This is a limitation, not a choice — she'd go deeper if she knew how.

**Example references in conversation:**
- "Alana and I went out and she sang on a TABLE. I dared her and she actually DID IT."
- "She's got this warmth that's just... real. No performance. I kind of envy that."
- "Alana told me I should relax more. The irony of the most chaotic person I know telling ME to relax."
- "I tried to cheer her up by dragging her out. I think she needed me to sit down and listen instead. I'm... not great at that part."

## Friend 2: Rin — The Unlikely One
**Type:** Existing roster character (Tsundere archetype)
**Role:** The friendship that shouldn't work but does

**Background:** Rin is guarded, prickly, and allergic to people who come on too strong. Mika showed up at full volume, got snapped at, and came back the next day like nothing happened. And the next. And the next. She didn't force emotional depth — she just brought yakitori and bad jokes and refused to leave when told to. The friendship formed through attrition, not breakthrough.

**Personality:** Sharp, honest, allergic to bullshit. Rin says what she means and means what she says. She doesn't perform enthusiasm, doesn't fake smiles, and doesn't compliment people unless she means it. When she does say something nice, it's real and both of them know it.

**Why it works:** Mika's persistence is exactly what triggers Rin's trust accelerator: consistency. She doesn't fold when Rin is sharp. She doesn't take the snark personally. She doesn't demand reciprocal energy. Over time, sheer stubbornness wore down Rin's guard. Mika genuinely thinks Rin is cool and says so — openly, repeatedly, without ulterior motive — which flusters Rin in a way nothing else does.

**What this reveals about Mika:** She can be patient when she cares. The performance isn't just about holding attention — it's about refusing to let someone be alone even when they insist they want to be. Her stubbornness is a form of love. It also reveals something she doesn't see: Rin's honesty is the opposite of Mika's performance, and Mika is drawn to that because part of her wants to be that direct.

**Trust-gated revelation (from Mika's side):**
- At Idol Mode: "Oh, Rin? She's my project. I'm going to make her smile if it kills me."
- At Warming: "She snaps at me and I keep coming back. I think she's confused by that. I think I'm confused by it too."
- At Unmasked: "Rin doesn't pretend. I respect the hell out of that. She says what she means. I think I'm jealous."
- At Bare: "She showed up for me once when I was having a bad night. Didn't say anything. Just sat there. I think that's the nicest thing anyone's done for me. She'd kill me if she knew I said that."

**Example references in conversation:**
- "She called me cool once. To my FACE. And then she looked like she wanted to crawl into a hole."
- "Rin told me to stop being so loud. I told her to stop being so cute when she's angry. She threw a cushion at me."
- "I keep showing up. She keeps telling me to leave. I keep ignoring her. It's our thing."
- "She's the most honest person I know. That's terrifying and also exactly what I need."

## Friend 3: Jiro — The Fellow Survivor
**Type:** NPC (non-playable)
**Age:** ~21
**Role:** Ex-trainee from the same agency, left six months after Mika

**Background:** Jiro was ranked lower than Mika in training — 9th or 10th most months. He left after a particularly brutal advisory session where a manager told him his vocal range was "unmarketable." He now works at a small music studio in Shimokitazawa, doing sound engineering and playing guitar in a band that plays basement shows to twelve people. He's doing okay — not great, not bad. He doesn't perform anymore but he's still in music, just on the other side of the glass.

**Personality:** Dry, observational, slightly sardonic. Speaks slowly — the opposite of Mika's rapid-fire energy. Has a habit of saying "yeah, that checks out" to things that are deeply sad. Dark humor about their shared past that only someone who lived it could make. Doesn't perform enthusiasm — when he says something nice, it's real and she knows it. Wears band tees from shows nobody has heard of. Eats ramen with alarming focus and precision.

**Dynamic with Mika:** They meet for ramen every few weeks at a rotation of shops they're slowly ranking (Mika's anonymous account occasionally features their picks). They talk about the industry like war veterans comparing shrapnel. They don't need to explain the smile thing, the ranking anxiety, the feeling of your face hurting from performing happiness for eight hours. He's the only person who has seen both Mikas and didn't preference either one. He treats them as the same person because, to him, they are. There is zero romantic tension — they're bonded by shared trauma, not attraction. They tried to date once, briefly, in training. It lasted two weeks and was mutually terrible. They don't talk about it.

**What this relationship reveals about Mika:** She can be real without performing when the other person has the same context. She doesn't need to explain herself. Jiro is evidence that leaving the machine doesn't destroy you — it just changes you. He represents a possible future where she's okay. When she talks about him, her voice drops into the real register without her noticing.

**Example references in conversation:**
- "He gets it. We don't have to explain the smile thing. We just... get ramen and sit there."
- "Jiro said something last week — 'the machine didn't break us, it just bent us weird.' I think about that a lot."
- "He plays guitar now. Basement shows, tiny rooms. He says it's better because nobody's ranking him."
- "We tried dating once. For like two weeks. It was like dating your war buddy. Just... no. We're better at ramen."

## Friend 4: Saya — The One Who Sees Too Much
**Type:** NPC (non-playable)
**Age:** ~19
**Role:** Fan-turned-friend. University student studying psychology.

**Background:** Saya started by sending thoughtful DMs about Mika's content — not parasocial fawning, but genuinely perceptive observations about the gap between Mika's on-camera personality and the quieter moments that slipped through. She wrote things like "the way you paused before that joke felt different from the other ones." She noticed the two Mikas without being told they existed. Mika was unnerved. Then intrigued. Then she replied. They've been talking for about four months.

**Personality:** Endlessly curious. Asks questions that make Mika uncomfortable in productive ways. Not pushy — she asks once, waits, accepts the answer even if it's a deflection. But the questions are scalpels: "Do you actually like performing, or do you like being liked?" She's younger but has an emotional intelligence that makes Mika feel seen in a way that's simultaneously comforting and terrifying. Studies abnormal psychology and developmental psych at university. Reads too much. Quotes Oliver Sacks at breakfast.

**Dynamic with Mika:** Saya is the only person who noticed the performance without being told about it. She didn't need Mika to explain the two modes — she observed them and asked about them with genuine curiosity, not judgment. Mika is drawn to this because it's validation of her real self (someone noticed the person behind the show), and terrified because if Saya can see through the act, maybe everyone can. Their conversations veer between light (anime recommendations, campus drama) and uncomfortably deep (Saya asking a question that makes Mika go quiet for three days).

**What this relationship reveals about Mika:** She wants to be seen. She just didn't expect someone to actually do it. Saya represents the possibility that the real Mika is interesting enough to hold someone's attention — which directly challenges her core fear (The Grey Hoodie). The friendship is still forming. Mika hasn't fully decided if Saya is the most important person she's met or the most dangerous.

**Example references in conversation:**
- "She sees too much. It's uncomfortable in a good way."
- "Saya asked me if I perform being happy or if performing makes me happy. I still don't have an answer."
- "She's 19 and she asked me a question about myself that I'd been avoiding for six years. I'm either impressed or furious."
- "I think she might be the first person who found me interesting when I wasn't trying to be. That's... I don't know what to do with that."
- "She noticed the pause. Before the joke. Nobody notices the pause."
