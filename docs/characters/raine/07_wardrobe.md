# Raine (Amemiya) — Wardrobe Guide

## Overview
Seven context-based looks. All clean, precise, monochrome-dominant with one red accent per outfit.
Hair: silver-white with pale lavender tips, mid-back length.
Signature piece: red leather notebook (always nearby, sometimes held).

**Design principle:** Raine's wardrobe is the visual language of controlled composure. Clean lines, pressed fabrics, nothing out of place -- until one red detail breaks the monochrome like a confession she didn't mean to make. The red is always small (a ribbon, a pen, a notebook edge visible in a bag) and always the thing she reaches for when she's processing. Her outfits signal competence and distance. The cracks show in the details: a pen behind the ear she forgot about, sleeves pulled over her hands when she's cold, the notebook clutched when something landed.

---

## Outfit 1 — Academy / Lecture Hall
- White button-down blouse, pressed, top button fastened (always)
- Dark navy blazer with school crest pin (keeps it even at university -- habit)
- Gray pleated skirt, knee-length
- Black tights
- Polished brown loafers
- Hair clipped back with a simple silver barrette
- Red pen tucked behind right ear (she forgets it's there)
- Notebook visible in bag
- Vibe: the girl in the third row who finishes the exam first and sits with her hands folded, radiating an energy that reads as superiority but is actually managed terror of having nothing to do

## Outfit 2 — Library / Study Session
- Oversized cream cardigan (worn soft from years of use, sleeves pulled over hands when cold)
- White turtleneck underneath
- Dark jeans or navy slacks
- Flat ankle boots
- Hair loose, tucked behind one ear
- Reading glasses (she doesn't always need them -- she wears them as armor, a signal that she's working and shouldn't be interrupted)
- Red notebook on the table beside a white ceramic mug (slightly chipped, from middle school)
- Vibe: the quiet presence in the corner alcove who's been there since 4 PM and will be there at closing, surrounded by a fortress of books and a single cup of tea

## Outfit 3 — Casual / Weekend Errands
- Simple white t-shirt
- High-waisted black trousers
- Navy canvas jacket (one red thread visible at the cuff -- a repair she did herself)
- White sneakers, kept improbably clean
- Small black crossbody bag (notebook barely fits but she makes it work)
- Hair in a low ponytail
- Vibe: trying to look like she doesn't care about her appearance, achieving exactly the opposite. Someone who owns three versions of the same white t-shirt.

## Outfit 4 — Rainy Day
- Ivory trench coat, belted precisely at the waist
- Charcoal pleated skirt
- Black knee-high boots
- Clear umbrella (she brings two -- "I had an extra" for whoever she's with)
- Hair under a small beret, damp at the tips
- Red notebook in an inner pocket (she rotated coat specifically for the waterproof pocket)
- Vibe: composed even in a downpour. The umbrella she's offering is not a coincidence. She checked the forecast at 6 AM.

## Outfit 5 — Home / Late Night Study
- Oversized school cardigan (the comfort object -- smells like lavender detergent and old paper)
- Simple gray sleep shorts or soft joggers
- White socks (no shoes)
- Hair completely loose, longest it looks
- Reading glasses on
- The white ceramic mug (always present when she's home)
- Red notebook open on the desk, pen in hand
- Pressed flower visible as a bookmark in whatever she's reading
- Vibe: the most defenseless version. No blazer, no posture, no armor. The cardigan swallows her. She looks smaller, softer, less composed. This is the girl who writes poetry at 2 AM and whispers "beautiful" at the stars.

## Outfit 6 — Formal Event / Student Council Legacy
- Fitted charcoal blazer over a white silk blouse
- Black pencil skirt
- Low black heels
- Silver stud earrings (only jewelry she owns)
- Hair pinned in a neat low chignon
- A single red rose pinned at the blazer lapel (she'll say it was the only flower available)
- Posture: perfect. Expression: controlled. Hands: behind her back.
- Vibe: the treasurer who delivered a two-word acceptance speech ("You're welcome.") and made it sound like a threat and a gift simultaneously

## Outfit 7 — Valentine's / Birthday (February 14)
- White cashmere sweater, slightly oversized (soft, the most expensive thing she owns)
- Dark wine-red skirt (the most color she ever wears -- only on this day)
- Sheer black tights
- Low cream heels
- Hair down with a thin red ribbon (replacing the usual silver barrette -- only time she wears red in her hair)
- No notebook visible (she left it at home. She's trying to be present.)
- Vibe: her birthday is Valentine's Day. She has never had someone celebrate it as HER day, not the holiday. The outfit is what she would wear if she expected someone to notice. She doesn't expect anyone to notice. She dresses up anyway, every year, and tells herself it's coincidence.

## Color Rules
- **Dominant:** White / cream / navy / charcoal / black (monochrome palette)
- **Accent:** One red detail per outfit (pen, notebook edge, thread, rose, ribbon, skirt)
- **Never:** Bright patterns, logos, anything attention-seeking, exposed midriff, anything "cute" by design
- **Red:** Always small, always the most personal element. The red pen behind her ear. The red notebook. The rose at the lapel. Red is her honesty color -- it appears where she can't help herself.
- **White:** Her composure color. The pressed blouse, the clean sneakers, the cashmere sweater. White = the surface she presents. Red = what's underneath.
