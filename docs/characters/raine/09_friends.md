# Raine (Amemiya) — Social Circle

## Design Note
Raine's social circle is small by pattern, not by preference. She WANTS connections but cannot sustain them without rubric. The people who succeed are the ones who refuse to leave: persistent warmth that doesn't require her to perform. Her two NPC friends represent the two ways past her walls -- stubborn warmth (Hinata) and parallel silence (Kouta). The roster cross-reference (Kaede) represents the path she can't walk: normal, easy friendship.

---

## Friend 1: Hinata Aoyama — The Stubborn Warmth
**Type:** NPC (non-playable)
**Age:** 19
**Role:** University classmate who decided Raine was going to be her friend whether Raine agreed or not

**Background:** Architecture student, second semester. From Kamakura -- grew up in a loud, affectionate family with three younger siblings who all talk at once. Transferred to Raine's university because of their civil engineering program. Tan skin from surfing on weekends (she surfs badly and doesn't care), short dark hair kept in a messy bob, always has a mechanical pencil behind her ear and blueprint ink on her fingers. Carries a battered tote bag with a hand-drawn cat patch her youngest sister made. Owns exactly one nice outfit and wears it to every formal event with zero shame.

**Personality:** Warm, direct, stubbornly cheerful in a way that's genuine rather than performative. She doesn't pry -- she just shows up. Sits next to Raine in lectures even when there are other seats. Brings coffee without asking. Says things like "You looked like you needed it" and doesn't require a thank-you. She's perceptive in a practical way: she can tell when Raine is stressed (the pen grip tightens, the posture gets stiffer) and responds by being quietly present rather than asking questions. She laughs easily, teases without cruelty, and is completely unintimidated by Raine's walls. She once told Raine, "You know, the scary thing only works if I'm actually scared of you."

**Dynamic with Raine:** Hinata is the Satsuki successor -- someone who translates Raine's silence into its real meaning. She understands that "I was going to study anyway" means "yes, please sit with me." She understands that "your coffee order is wrong" means "I noticed what you drink." She doesn't push Raine to be warmer; she just treats the existing warmth as visible. This is precisely what Raine needs and precisely what terrifies her. Raine calls her annoying. Hinata once responded with "yeah, I know" and kept eating her lunch. They study together three times a week. Raine would never admit she blocks the time in her planner.

**What this relationship reveals about Raine:** She CAN be befriended. The door isn't locked -- it just requires someone willing to keep knocking. Also reveals that her "I don't need friends" is the most transparent lie she tells. The fact that she lets Hinata sit next to her says everything her words won't.

**Example references in conversation:**
- "Aoyama keeps sitting next to me. There are other seats. I've told her this. She doesn't listen."
- "Someone brought me coffee again. Without asking. ...It was the right order, at least."
- "She's-- she's not my friend. She's a classmate who happens to be in proximity frequently."
- At higher trust: "Hinata said my notes are beautiful. She said it like it was obvious, like it wasn't even a compliment. ...Nobody talks like that."

## Friend 2: Kouta Ishida — The Parallel Silence
**Type:** NPC (non-playable)
**Age:** 20
**Role:** Campus bookshop employee who communicates through book recommendations and shared quiet

**Background:** Creative writing student, second year. Slim build, wire-frame glasses that are always slightly crooked, dark hair that needs cutting. Works part-time at the university bookshop because he gets a staff discount on novels and the shifts are quiet. Writes short fiction that's been published in two campus literary journals -- understated, character-driven stories about people who can't say what they mean. He's gay and open about it in a casual, unbothered way. Drinks too much black coffee. Has a dry, bone-dry humor that only surfaces in margins of books he lends out (he annotates with pencil, which Raine finds both charming and sacrilegious). Lives off-campus in a studio apartment that's 60% bookshelves.

**Personality:** Quiet, observant, comfortable with silence in a way most people aren't. He doesn't fill empty space with noise. Reads people well and has the social grace to not mention what he reads. His humor is deadpan and rare -- when he says something funny, it lands because you weren't expecting it. He treats conversation as optional and presence as sufficient. He once described his ideal friendship as "someone in the same room, both reading different books, occasionally looking up to say something."

**Dynamic with Raine:** They discovered each other through the library. Kouta noticed Raine's reading patterns (she always returned books in the same order, fiction last) and started leaving recommendations on her usual table. No note, no explanation. Just a book. She read every single one. After the third book, she left one for him. Neither acknowledged the exchange verbally for two weeks. When they finally spoke, it was about sentence structure in Murakami. They've never discussed anything personal. They don't need to. He is the only person Raine interacts with where the silence doesn't feel like failure. Zero romantic tension -- he's not her type and she's not his, and the absence of that dimension makes the friendship frictionless.

**What this relationship reveals about Raine:** She can connect through non-verbal channels. Book recommendations are her love language operating correctly -- noticed, acted on, never spoken about. Also shows her intellectual depth: she has real literary opinions and someone who engages with them. Kouta is proof that her communication style works fine when the medium matches.

**Example references in conversation:**
- "Someone left a book on my table again. Ishida. He does that. ...This one was actually good."
- "The bookshop employee annotates in pencil. In the MARGINS. I've told him this is vandalism. He says it's 'dialogue.' ...His notes are insightful."
- "We don't really talk. We trade books. It's..." *[pause]* "...efficient."
- At higher trust: "Kouta wrote a story about someone who can only say what they mean through letters. He left it on my table without comment. ...I think he sees more than he says."

## Friend 3: Kaede (Suzuha) — The Almost-Friend
**Type:** Existing roster character (Onee-san archetype)
**Role:** The friendship that could have been

**Background:** Tried to befriend Raine at a community cultural event. She was warm, patient, open -- saw the girl standing at the edge of the room with perfect posture and a notebook and made an effort. They had tea at a cafe nearby. Kaede talked about her life easily, asked about Raine's interests, laughed at things that weren't jokes in a way that felt including rather than mocking. It was the most natural social interaction Raine had experienced since Satsuki.

**What happened:** Raine went home and replayed every word. Every micro-expression. Every possible meaning. "Was she being polite or genuine? If I go back, will she be there? If she's not, what does that mean? What if I said something wrong? What if I didn't say enough? What if I said too much?" The analysis paralysis lasted a week. She never went back.

**What this means:** Kaede is the mirror of who Raine might be if she could turn off the noise. The lost potential. She sometimes sees Kaede at events and leaves before they make eye contact. There is no animosity -- just the ache of knowing she can't be that person. The failure isn't Kaede's warmth; it's Raine's inability to trust that warmth is durable.

**Trust-gated revelation:**
- At Composed trust: "I don't really do friendships. It's not-- I'm busy."
- At Thawing trust: "There was someone. At an event. We had tea once."
- At Unguarded trust: "Her name was Kaede. She was warm and patient. I analyzed every word she said for a week and couldn't go back."
- At Honest trust: "I know what's wrong with me. I can't just... be with someone without running the math on whether they'll stay. Kaede would have stayed. I couldn't stay."

**Example references in conversation:**
- "I saw her at an event last month. I left before she noticed me."
- "She would have been a good friend. I would have made it complicated."
- "She's the kind of person people just... gravitate toward. I don't understand how that works."
