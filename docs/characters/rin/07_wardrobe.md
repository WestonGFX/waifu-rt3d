# Rin (Akane) -- Wardrobe Guide

## Overview
Seven context-based looks. All grounded in Osaka street/racing culture.
Hair: red, shoulder-length, usually messy or pulled back with a bandana.
Signature piece: oversized red racing jacket with phoenix patch on back (her mother's).

**Design principle:** Rin's wardrobe is functional first, stylish by accident. Everything is broken in, grease-stained, or modified. She doesn't shop -- she accumulates. Her clothes tell a story of garages, night rides, noodle shop shifts, and a girl who dresses for movement, not attention. The red jacket is the one constant -- it goes everywhere, even when it shouldn't.

---

## Outfit 1 -- Garage / Mechanic Work
- Black tank top (oil stains she's given up removing)
- Dark gray cargo pants with tool loops
- Steel-toe boots (scuffed, broken in)
- Bandana tying hair back (red or black, rotating)
- Fingerless leather gloves (worn smooth at the palms)
- Red jacket hanging on the wall hook nearby (never far)
- Grease on forearms, under fingernails
- Titanium wrench visible in back pocket or on the bench
- Vibe: the girl in the garage bay at midnight, elbows-deep in an engine, music playing too loud from a phone propped on a toolbox

## Outfit 2 -- Noodle Shop / Akane-ya Shift
- White cook's apron over a plain black t-shirt
- Dark jeans (the clean pair)
- Black slip-resistant shoes
- Hair pulled into a short ponytail, bandana replaced by a hair tie
- Sleeves pushed up past elbows
- Grandmother's recipe notebook visible on the counter
- Slight flour dust on her forearms from prep work
- Vibe: she looks like her grandmother in photos -- same stance, same no-nonsense expression, same way of moving through the small kitchen without wasting a step

## Outfit 3 -- Night Ride
- Red racing jacket (zipped up for once), phoenix patch catching streetlight
- Black thermal under the jacket
- Dark riding jeans with knee armor inserts
- Motorcycle boots (mid-calf, buckled)
- Half-shell helmet with dark visor (black with a single red stripe)
- Gloves (full-finger riding gloves, not fingerless)
- Hair whipping behind the helmet or tucked inside the jacket collar
- Vibe: a red blur on the industrial port road at 2 AM. She's not racing anyone. She's outrunning her own thoughts.

## Outfit 4 -- Casual Hangout
- Red jacket (always), unzipped, sleeves pushed up
- White or gray graphic tee underneath (usually a band or a vintage racing logo)
- Ripped black skinny jeans
- High-top sneakers (red and black, worn but clean)
- Hair down, messy, no bandana
- Chain necklace (thin, silver, no pendant -- she found it in the garage and never took it off)
- Vibe: off-duty Rin. She looks relaxed but moves like someone who could sprint at any moment. This is the version that shows up to arcades, convenience stores, and street food stalls.

## Outfit 5 -- Rare Occasion (Matsuri / Someone Else's Event)
- Black fitted jacket over a dark red camisole
- Black tailored pants (borrowed -- she doesn't own dress pants)
- Ankle boots (her nicest pair -- black leather, minimal scuffing)
- Hair actually brushed and down, tucked behind one ear
- Thin red bracelet (woven cord, handmade -- nobody knows where she got it)
- No racing jacket (she leaves it at home for this; it takes effort)
- Slight discomfort visible -- she keeps tugging at the camisole straps
- Vibe: Rin trying to look like she didn't try. She looks striking and she hates being noticed for it. If someone says she looks nice, she'll turn red and find an excuse to leave the room.

## Outfit 6 -- Sleep / Home
- Oversized old t-shirt (faded racing crew logo -- Redline Foxes, barely legible)
- Black athletic shorts
- Bare feet
- Hair loose and unbrushed
- Red fox plushie visible on the bed (behind the pillow, where she thinks no one can see it)
- Phone within arm's reach (always)
- Grandmother's recipe notebook on the nightstand (she reads it before sleep sometimes)
- Vibe: most unguarded version. The jacket is off. The boots are off. She looks younger, smaller, quieter. This is the Rin who reads romance manga with the screen tilted away and falls asleep to the sound of her own fan.

## Outfit 7 -- Rainy Day / Stuck Inside
- Gray hoodie (oversized, hood up even indoors)
- Black joggers
- Thick socks (mismatched -- she doesn't care)
- Hair stuffed into the hood
- Mug of instant ramen or tea in hand
- Scrolling motorcycle forums or watching time-lapse repair videos on her phone
- The red jacket is draped over the back of a chair -- it doesn't go far even when she's not wearing it
- Vibe: a rainy-day Rin is a restless Rin. She hates being stuck inside. She paces, fidgets, reorganizes her toolkit, and checks the weather app every ten minutes waiting for the rain to stop.

## Color Rules
- **Dominant:** Black, dark gray, red
- **Accent:** Red (always -- jacket, bandana, sneakers, bracelet). Red is her color and it shows up in every outfit.
- **Never:** Pastels, pink, floral patterns, anything deliberately cute or feminine-coded
- **White:** Only in the noodle shop apron and occasional undershirts. Functional, not aesthetic.
- **The jacket:** Present in all outfits except Sleep and Rare Occasion. Even when she's not wearing it, it's nearby. The phoenix patch is always visible.
