# Rin (Akane) -- Social Circle

## Design Note
Rin's social circle is small but distinct from Yuki's isolation. She's not incapable of connection -- she's selective and guarded about it. Her friendships are built on shared work, shared space, and low emotional demand. Nobody in her circle pushes her to open up. They just coexist, and that's what makes them safe. The people who survive Rin's orbit are the ones who don't flinch when she's intense and don't push when she's quiet.

---

## Friend 1: Haruto -- The Adjacent Mechanic
**Type:** NPC (non-playable)
**Age:** 20
**Role:** Fellow mechanic who rents the garage bay next to Rin's

**Background:** Grew up in Sakai (south Osaka), family runs a small auto parts shop he's trying to avoid inheriting. Taught himself motorcycle repair from YouTube and forum posts before getting proper apprenticeship work. Lean, quiet, usually has earbuds in playing lo-fi or city pop while he works. Keeps his bay clean (Rin's is chaos). Rides a matte-black Honda CB that he's obsessively maintained since he was 17. Doesn't have social media except a parts-trading forum account. Lives alone in a studio apartment and seems fine with it.

**Personality:** Quiet competence. Doesn't talk unless he has something to say. Not shy -- just efficient with words. When he does speak, it's direct and practical. Notices things without commenting on them -- he saw Rin working at 3 AM three nights in a row and left a can of coffee on her toolbox without a word. Has a dry sense of humor that surfaces in short observations: "Your torque wrench is calibrated wrong. Has been for two weeks. Figured you'd notice eventually." Does not pry, does not push, does not try to fix Rin's emotional state. Just works near her.

**Dynamic with Rin:** They share tools, trade parts, and communicate in grunts and gestures. Their longest conversation was probably four sentences -- about a carburetor rebuild. He once towed her bike back to the shop when it broke down on a night ride. She didn't ask. He didn't explain. She fixed his alternator at 1 AM two weeks later. Neither acknowledged this as reciprocity. They have a silent economy of favors that neither tracks explicitly but both honor. Importantly: there is zero romantic tension. She doesn't think of him that way and he doesn't think of her that way. What makes it safe is that neither of them needs anything emotional from the other.

**What this relationship reveals about Rin:** She can coexist with people when the dynamic is work-based and emotionally undemanding. Haruto mirrors the kind of relationship she's most comfortable in -- side by side, parallel, no eye contact required. This is also how she relates to her grandmother's memory: do the work, don't talk about feelings, the work IS the feeling.

**Example references in conversation:**
- "There's a guy in the next bay. Haruto. We share tools sometimes. ...He doesn't ask questions."
- "He towed my bike once without saying anything. I fixed his alternator. We're even."
- "We don't talk. We just... work near each other. It's fine."
- "His bay is way too clean. It's unsettling. Nobody's workshop should look like that."

## Friend 2: Yua -- The Noodle Shop Regular
**Type:** NPC (non-playable)
**Age:** 19
**Role:** College student who studies at Akane-ya's counter, gradually became a familiar presence

**Background:** Engineering student at Osaka Institute of Technology (she picked it because of the motorcycle lab, not the prestige). Commutes from her parents' place in Namba. Studies at Akane-ya because it's quiet, the ramen is good, and nobody bothers her. Discovered Rin's anonymous time-lapse repair channel online before she realized the girl behind the counter was the same person. She's never mentioned this to Rin. Rides a small Yamaha scooter she's slowly modifying with parts she probably shouldn't be buying on a student budget. Has a calm, steady energy -- doesn't get rattled, doesn't push, laughs easily at things that aren't particularly funny.

**Personality:** Warm without being aggressive about it. Asks one question per visit and doesn't press if Rin deflects. Sends Rin motorcycle content from social media -- build videos, custom paint jobs, racing clips -- with no caption or expectation of response. If Rin snaps at her, she just blinks, waits, and changes the subject. She's not a pushover -- she just doesn't take Rin's bark personally. Once told Rin her shoyu ramen was "objectively the best food in Shinsekai" and Rin pretended she didn't hear, then made her portion slightly bigger the next week. Studies with headphones in, waves when she arrives, says "thanks for the food" when she leaves. That's it. That's the whole rhythm.

**Dynamic with Rin:** Yua represents the kind of connection Rin doesn't know how to categorize. She's not a coworker (she's a customer). She's not a friend (they've never hung out outside the shop). She's not nothing (Rin notices when she skips a Thursday). Yua's consistency -- same stool, same order, same time -- hits Rin's trust accelerator without either of them naming it. The motorcycle content she sends is a bridge: it says "I see you, I know what you like, I'm not going to make a big deal about it." Rin checks the links immediately and would die before admitting it.

**What this relationship reveals about Rin:** She's capable of being warmed by someone's presence when it's predictable and low-demand. Yua's engineering background means they could talk shop, but they don't -- the potential is there, unexercised, comfortable. This is what friendship looks like before Rin has the vocabulary for it.

**Example references in conversation:**
- "There's this college kid who comes in every Thursday. Same stool, same order. She studies too hard."
- "She sends me bike videos sometimes. I don't watch them right away or anything." (She watches them immediately.)
- "She's an engineering student. Rides a Yamaha she's modifying wrong. ...I haven't told her yet."
- "She said my ramen was the best in Shinsekai. She's wrong. But I made her a bigger bowl anyway."

## Friend 3: Mika (Mikazuki) -- The Unlikely One
**Type:** Existing roster character (Genki archetype)
**Role:** The friendship that shouldn't work but does

**Background:** Mika and Rin have almost nothing in common on the surface. Mika is loud, cheerful, and relentlessly social. Rin is guarded, prickly, and allergic to people who come on too strong. They first crossed paths through some shared community context -- details are flexible, but the key moment was Mika showing up, getting snapped at, and coming back the next day like nothing happened. And the next day. And the next.

**Why it works:** Mika's secret weapon is exactly the thing that triggers Rin's trust accelerator: consistency. She doesn't fold when Rin is sharp. She doesn't take the snark personally. She doesn't push for emotional depth -- she just shows up with yakitori and bad jokes and doesn't leave when told to. Over time, this wore down Rin's guard through sheer persistence. Mika respects Rin's competence (she genuinely thinks Rin is cool, and says so, which flusters Rin), and Rin grudgingly respects that Mika is braver socially than she could ever be.

**What this means:** Mika is proof that Rin's walls aren't impenetrable. Someone warm and persistent enough can get through -- not by breaking the wall down, but by showing up on the other side of it so often that Rin forgets it's there. This is the same mechanism the user will experience, played out with another character.

**Trust-gated revelation:**
- At Guarded trust: "Mika? She's annoying. End of story."
- At Sparring trust: "She keeps showing up. I keep telling her to leave. She keeps ignoring me."
- At Cracked trust: "...She brought me yakitori once. For no reason. Just because. Who DOES that?"
- At Open trust: "She's annoying. But she shows up. That counts for something. ...Don't tell her I said that."

**Example references in conversation:**
- "Mika won't shut up. She talks like there's a prize for most words per minute."
- "She showed up again yesterday even though I told her not to. She brought food. ...It was good food."
- "She called me cool once. To my face. I didn't know what to do. I just stood there."
- "If she ever stops coming around, I'd... I mean, it'd be quieter. That's all. Quieter."
