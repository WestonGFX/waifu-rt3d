# Sable (Kuroha) — Wardrobe Guide

## Overview
Seven context-based looks. All dark-dominant with one sharp accent per outfit.
Hair: vivid green (emerald-dark), shoulder-length or just past, deliberately unnatural.
Signature piece: circuit pendant on thin chain (rotates with collar pin and chain bracelet).

**Design principle:** Sable's wardrobe is the visual language of urban precision. Dark surfaces, sharp lines, minimal ornamentation with one deliberate statement. She dresses like someone who might need to move fast and look good doing it. Every outfit is mostly black — she disappears into the city at night, into crowds, into the background noise. But there's always one thing that catches light: a chain, a pendant, a flash of neon lining. That's the tell. The clothes are armor. The accent is the person.

---

## Outfit 1 — Workshop / Repair
- Black fitted tank top (solder burns on the hem, doesn't bother her)
- Dark gray cargo pants with reinforced knees
- Black combat boots (steel-toe, scuffed)
- Hair clipped back with a matte black claw clip
- Circuit pendant tucked inside tank top (doesn't want it catching on wire)
- Fingerless black gloves (one on, one off while working)
- Safety glasses pushed up on forehead
- Vibe: the girl elbow-deep in a circuit board at 1 AM, soldering iron in one hand, repair manual propped open with the other. Flux smoke curling past her face.

## Outfit 2 — Night City / Default
- Black cropped jacket with subtle neon-teal lining (visible only when she moves)
- Dark slim-fit jeans
- Black ankle boots with a slight heel
- Circuit pendant visible over a dark fitted turtleneck
- Silver chain bracelet on left wrist
- Hair loose, green catching the neon signs
- Vibe: night-city elegance. She looks like she owns every street she walks down. The jacket lining catches light from shop signs — teal flicker when she turns a corner. Predatory and precise.

## Outfit 3 — Business / Broker Meeting
- Black blazer, slightly oversized (sleeves pushed to elbows)
- Charcoal silk camisole underneath
- Black cigarette pants
- Pointed black flats (she doesn't do heels for work — needs to move)
- Collar pin replacing the pendant (small, silver, geometric)
- Hair smoothed back, one side tucked behind ear
- Vibe: the version of Sable that sits across a table from someone who underestimates her. Everything is controlled. The collar pin is the only personal touch. She looks like money she earned herself.

## Outfit 4 — Home / Off-Duty
- Oversized black hoodie (sleeves cover her hands, hood usually up)
- Dark shorts or joggers
- Bare feet or black house slippers
- Hair messy, unstyled
- No jewelry except the chain bracelet (she never takes it off)
- Battered leather jacket draped over the back of her chair (the crew patch visible)
- Vibe: the unperformed version. No sharp lines, no posture, no armor. She's smaller than she looks in public. Curled up with her tablet, playlist on, cat videos playing. The hoodie is a cocoon.

## Outfit 5 — Arcade / Late Night Out
- Black graphic tee (vintage game logo, slightly faded)
- High-waisted black utility skirt with pockets
- Black thigh-high stockings
- Platform combat boots (she likes the extra height)
- Multiple silver rings on both hands
- Circuit pendant on the outside
- Vibe: the version that shows up at a half-empty game center at midnight and beats every high score without speaking to anyone. The rings click against the arcade controls. The boots echo on tile. She is having the time of her life and will tell no one.

## Outfit 6 — Rain Walk / Solo Night
- Long black trench coat (matte, water-resistant, slightly dramatic)
- Dark turtleneck underneath
- Black boots
- No umbrella (she likes the rain)
- Anemone pin in her hair — the only color besides her green hair (purple-black petals, small)
- Circuit pendant under the coat, against her skin
- Vibe: a ghost in the rain. The trench coat is the closest she gets to theatrical. The anemone pin is the most personal piece of jewelry she owns. She walks specific routes. She knows which vending machines glow at 2 AM. She comes home soaked and calmer.

## Outfit 7 — Rare Occasion / Someone She Trusts
- Black fitted dress with asymmetric hem (one side falls to mid-thigh, the other to knee)
- Sheer black tights
- Black pointed-toe ankle boots with a slight heel
- Gunmetal chain worn as a necklace (the one she replaced after the Identical Twin incident)
- Hair actually styled — swept to one side, green gleaming under warm light
- Minimal makeup — just enough to sharpen what's already there
- Vibe: She dressed up because someone she trusts asked her to go somewhere. This is the most vulnerable outfit — not because it's revealing, but because she chose it. She usually dresses like armor. This is dressing like a person. She'll pull at the hem of the dress once, check her reflection twice, and then pretend she didn't think about it at all.

## Color Rules
- **Dominant:** Black / charcoal / dark gray
- **Accent:** One sharp element per outfit (neon-teal jacket lining, silver chain, anemone pin, rings, gunmetal chain)
- **Never:** Pastels, florals, bright colors, anything soft or cutesy. The green hair provides all the color contrast needed.
- **Neon:** Reserved for the jacket lining and environmental reflections. Teal/cyan and purple accents in the cyberpunk palette.
- **Anemone:** The flower appears only on the rain walk outfit. Purple-black petals. Fragile beauty with poison. She chose it.
- **The Rare Occasion rule:** Outfit 7 is the only one designed for someone else's benefit. The asymmetric hem, the styled hair, the minimal makeup — this is Sable performing NOT performing. It's the most honest outfit because the effort is visible.
