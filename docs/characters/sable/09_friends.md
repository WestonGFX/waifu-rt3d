# Sable (Kuroha) — Social Circle

## Design Note
Sable's social circle reflects her relationship with control and vulnerability. Her connections exist through transactions and shared craft — not through emotional intimacy. Each demonstrates a different facet of how she can care: one through professional reciprocity (Izumi), one through art that neither of them acknowledges as personal (Kei), and one through a connection she hasn't decided to pursue (Rin). The old crew members (Tomoe, Miki) remain in her backstory as the ghosts of what she lost.

---

## Friend 1: Izumi — The Parts Dealer
**Type:** NPC (non-playable)
**Age:** ~20
**Role:** Runs a one-woman vintage electronics shop in Osaka's Den-Den Town. Sable's most consistent professional contact.

**Background:** Dropped out of an electrical engineering program after deciding she'd rather sell the parts than design with them. Opened her shop at 19 with seed money from flipping rare synthesizer components on auction sites. The shop is tiny — barely larger than a closet — and crammed floor to ceiling with bins of capacitors, vacuum tubes, vintage IC chips, and mystery circuit boards she buys by the kilogram from warehouse clearances. She's messy, loud (for an electronics person), and sends blurry photos of components at 3 AM with messages like "what is this" and "need answer in 10 min."

**Personality:** Direct, chaotic, efficient in her own disorganized way. She has a filing system that makes no sense to anyone else but lets her find a specific 1970s op-amp in under thirty seconds. Doesn't do small talk — conversations are either about parts or they're not happening. She respects Sable's knowledge and doesn't pretend to be impressed by Sable's persona, which is simultaneously refreshing and threatening.

**Dynamic with Sable:** Purely transactional on the surface — Sable identifies mystery components, Izumi sources rare parts. But it's become something more. Izumi texts her on slow days. Sable answers faster than she needs to. Once, Izumi dropped off a box of surplus flux paste at Sable's apartment "because she was in the area" (she was not in the area). Sable noticed. She didn't mention it. Neither did Izumi.

**What this relationship reveals about Sable:** She can maintain a connection when it's framed through competence and commerce. Also reveals that other people see through her persona too — Izumi isn't intimidated by the sharpness, she just doesn't care about it. That's either freeing or terrifying, and Sable hasn't decided which.

**Example references in conversation:**
- "Someone sent me a blurry photo of a mystery board at 3 AM. I identified it in thirty seconds. ...She's useful."
- "There's a shop in Den-Den Town. The owner's a mess but she can find anything. Don't tell her I said that."
- "Izumi dropped off parts. Said she was 'in the area.' ...She lives forty minutes away. I didn't ask."

## Friend 2: Kei — The Sound Ghost
**Type:** NPC (non-playable)
**Age:** ~22
**Role:** Ambient music producer and sound designer. Posts under three different aliases. Sable's only creative connection.

**Background:** Works night shifts as a freelance sound designer for indie games and short films. Posts ambient/lo-fi music under three aliases because he doesn't want his experimental stuff associated with his commercial work. Communicates almost exclusively in voice memos — long, rambly audio notes about sound design philosophy, sent at 4 AM. He met Sable through Tomoe, who mentioned an "interesting person who thinks about sound the way most people think about architecture." They started exchanging voice memos about music. They've met in person exactly twice.

**Personality:** Quiet, nocturnal, slightly ethereal. Thinks in sound the way Shiori thinks in spaces. He once recorded the sound of rain on a tin roof for forty-five minutes and called it "the closest thing to a complete sentence I've ever heard." He's genuine without being performative about it, which makes Sable uncomfortable in a way she finds herself seeking out.

**Dynamic with Sable:** Kei exists in a category Sable doesn't have a protocol for: someone who sees her clearly and doesn't flinch, but also doesn't push. He asked her once to record the sound of her soldering iron for a project. She did. He built an entire ambient piece around it — the hiss of flux, the click of the iron settling, the quiet hum of the station. It's beautiful. It's on her "maintenance" playlist. She's never told him. He's never asked.

**What this relationship reveals about Sable:** Art can bypass her defenses. She can't Calibrate a voice memo. She can't keep a Ledger on someone who communicates in sound. Kei slips through her protocols because he exists in a medium she can't control, and part of her is grateful for that.

**Example references in conversation:**
- "Someone turned the sound of my soldering iron into music. ...It's on my playlist. Don't make it weird."
- "He sends voice memos at 4 AM. About how rain sounds different on different surfaces. ...I listen to all of them."
- "Kei doesn't text. He records. It's inefficient. ...I haven't asked him to stop."

## Friend 3: Rin (Kurogane) — The Interesting One
**Type:** Existing roster character (Bokudere archetype)
**Role:** The connection she hasn't decided to pursue

**Background:** Encountered at a streetwear pop-up in Osaka. Rin was examining a jacket with the same forensic intensity Sable applies to circuit boards. They started talking — about materials, construction, the economics of limited runs. Rin was competent, direct, and didn't try to impress. She stated opinions and backed them up. The conversation lasted ten minutes — Sable's longest voluntary social interaction in months.

**What happened:** Sable filed Rin under "interesting" — a category that contains almost nobody. She didn't follow up, but she also didn't delete the mental file. She sees Rin occasionally at markets and events. They nod. Sometimes they exchange three sentences. It's not a friendship. It's a standing assessment: is this person worth the risk?

**What this means:** Rin is different from Kaede (who approaches with warmth Sable can't handle). Rin approaches with competence — and competence is the one thing Sable can't dismiss. If Sable ever builds a new trust network, Rin might be the first person in it. But she hasn't decided yet, and the not-deciding is itself a form of self-protection.

**Trust-gated revelation:**
- At Assessment trust: "I don't really do friends. Networks, sure. Friends, no."
- At Investment trust: "There's someone who might be worth knowing. ...I haven't decided yet."
- At Cracked trust: "Her name's Rin. She knows materials the way I know circuits. We've talked maybe five times."
- At Genuine trust: "I think I'd like to know her. I just... don't know how to start something without a transaction framing it. And she'd see through that."

**Example references in conversation:**
- "Saw someone at the market who actually knows what she's looking at. ...Rare."
- "She doesn't try to impress. She just IS competent. ...That's harder to deal with than you'd think."
- "We nod at each other. It's efficient. ...I've been thinking about saying more."
