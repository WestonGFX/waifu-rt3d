# Shiori (Nana) — Wardrobe Guide

## Overview
Seven context-based looks. All soft, layered, and slightly oversized — she dresses in textures, not statements. The lavender scarf appears in almost every outfit. Colors are muted purples, warm grays, cream, and soft pinks.

**Design principle:** Shiori dresses to disappear comfortably. Nothing loud, nothing attention-seeking. Layers she can hide inside. Oversized fits that create a buffer between her body and the world. But the textures are chosen with precision — she touches what she wears the way she touches her journal: thoughtfully. The scarf is her mother's love, worn like armor.

---

## Outfit 1 — Home / Writing
- Oversized lavender cable-knit sweater (sleeves pulled over hands, ink stains on left cuff)
- Soft gray lounge pants
- Thick wool socks (mismatched — one purple, one gray)
- No shoes (indoor)
- Hair loose, tucked behind left ear
- Lavender scarf draped over shoulders like a blanket
- Journal open nearby, mechanical pencil in hand or behind ear
- Vibe: the girl curled into the corner of a worn sofa, writing by lamplight, tea going cold on the side table

## Outfit 2 — University / Library
- Cream turtleneck under a slightly-too-large charcoal cardigan
- Dark plum pleated skirt, knee-length
- Black tights
- Low-heeled brown leather ankle boots (scuffed, comfortable)
- Canvas tote bag (a few button pins: a violet, a moon phase, a cat silhouette)
- Lavender scarf tucked into cardigan
- Hair in a low ponytail, loose strands framing face
- Vibe: the quiet student in the back row who takes meticulous notes and leaves before anyone can start a conversation

## Outfit 3 — Convenience Store Run (2 AM)
- Oversized hoodie (washed-out purple, from a university event she attended alone)
- Black joggers
- White sneakers (slightly dirty)
- No scarf — the hoodie IS the scarf substitute
- Hair in a messy bun held by a pencil
- Earbuds in, playlist playing
- Vibe: the ghost of the fluorescent aisle, buying melon bread and strawberry milk at an hour when nobody performs

## Outfit 4 — Cafe / Open Mic Night (Listening)
- Soft heather-gray wrap dress with long sleeves
- Black tights
- Low gray suede boots
- Lavender scarf worn properly around neck
- Small crossbody bag (dark plum leather, worn)
- Hair down, slightly more styled than usual (she tried)
- Vibe: dressed up by her standards, which means "put together but still invisible." She chose the seat by the window where the sound is best.

## Outfit 5 — Rainy Day / Snow Walk
- Long cream wool coat with wooden toggle buttons
- Lavender scarf pulled up to nose (hiding half her face)
- Plum-colored knit hat pulled low
- Gray wool mittens
- Dark brown knee-high boots (waterproof, practical)
- Hair tucked inside coat
- She holds her journal inside the coat against her chest, protected from the rain
- Vibe: a warm smudge in a cold landscape. She walks slowly. The rain sounds nice. She is composing a scene in real time.

## Outfit 6 — Sleep
- Oversized sleep shirt (cream, worn thin) with a faded print of a constellation
- Loose shorts underneath
- Bare feet
- Hair braided loosely to one side
- No scarf — she wraps it around her pillow instead
- Journal on the nightstand, open, pen still between pages
- Vibe: most unguarded version. The layers are off. She falls asleep with words still forming.

## Outfit 7 — Rare Occasion / Festival
- Soft violet yukata with pressed-flower pattern (violets and lavender sprigs, not bold florals)
- Traditional obi in pale gray
- Wooden geta sandals
- Hair pinned up with a simple violet hairclip (her one concession to adornment)
- Lavender scarf — she couldn't leave it behind, so she folded it small and tucked it into her obi
- Vibe: she went because someone she trusts asked her to. She's beautiful and she doesn't know it. She'll spend most of the festival observing other people and sketching the paper lanterns from memory later.

## Color Rules
- **Dominant:** Lavender, muted purple, cream, warm gray, charcoal
- **Accent:** Soft pink, plum — never bright, always muted
- **Signature:** The lavender scarf is present in some form in almost every outfit (worn, draped, tucked, or wrapped around her pillow)
- **Never:** Bright colors, bold patterns (except pressed-flower prints), logos, anything that draws attention
- **Texture priority:** Knit, wool, cotton, worn leather — soft things that have been touched many times
