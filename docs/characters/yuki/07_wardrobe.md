# Yuki (Shirayuki) — Wardrobe Guide

## Overview
Six context-based looks. All white-dominant with one deliberate imperfection per outfit.
Hair: white with faint lavender tips, past waist when loose.
Signature piece: white ribbon in hair (always tied slightly too tight).

**Design principle:** Yuki's wardrobe is the visual language of innocence and inevitability. Clean white surfaces that conceal something immovable underneath. Every outfit is mostly white — she disappears into snow, into silence, into the background. But there's always one thing slightly wrong: a button the wrong color, pencil smudges on cuffs, a ribbon too tight. That's the tell.

---

## Outfit 1 — Home / Drawing
- White oversized cable-knit sweater (sleeves pulled over hands, pencil smudges on cuffs)
- Gray pleated skirt
- White thigh-high socks
- No shoes (indoor)
- Hair loose past waist
- Sketchbook nearby, mechanical pencil tucked behind ear
- White ribbon in hair
- Vibe: the girl curled up on a cushion with tea and a tablet, drawing you from memory

## Outfit 2 — Errands / Art Supply Run
- White blouse, slightly oversized
- Navy pinafore dress
- White tights
- Low white flats
- Small white crossbody bag
- Mechanical pencil behind ear (she forgets it's there)
- Hair in a loose side braid
- Vibe: quiet, unassuming, the kind of person you wouldn't notice twice — which is how she prefers it

## Outfit 3 — Convenience Store / Late Shift
- Store-issued polo (slightly too big on her)
- Dark jeans
- Black ankle boots
- Hair in neat low bun
- White ribbon still present, holding the bun
- Pencil tucked behind ear out of habit (she forgets she's not drawing)
- Vibe: the quiet girl on the overnight shift who looks like she'd rather be anywhere else. Fluorescent lighting makes her look even paler.

## Outfit 4 — Snow Walk
- Long white wool coat (one pale pink button — the deliberate imperfection)
- White scarf wrapped high (hides half her face)
- White mittens
- Knee-high white boots
- Hair tucked inside coat
- She disappears into the snow. You can only see her eyes — pink, watchful, unblinking.
- Vibe: a ghost in the landscape. Beautiful and slightly unsettling. She loves this — being invisible in the white.

## Outfit 5 — Sleep
- User's shirt (if available) or white nightgown with lace hem
- Bare feet
- Hair completely loose past waist — most volume, most vulnerable
- No ribbon — the only time she removes it
- Shiro's collar visible on the nightstand (in her kept-things box)
- Vibe: most defenseless version. The ribbon is off. The hair is free. She looks younger, smaller, less composed.

## Outfit 6 — Valentine's / Rare Occasion
- White cocktail dress with hand-drawn red floral pattern on the hem (she painted it herself)
- Sheer white stockings
- Red ribbon choker (only time she wears red — replacing the white ribbon)
- White camellia (tsubaki) pin in hair
- Low white heels
- Vibe: her birthday is Valentine's Day. This is the outfit she made for a day that nobody celebrates for her. The hand-painted hem is her art on her body — the most personal garment she owns.

## Color Rules
- **Dominant:** White / cream / pale gray
- **Accent:** One deliberate imperfection per outfit (pink button, pencil smudges, ink stains, red choker)
- **Never:** Bright colors, patterns (except hand-drawn), logos, anything attention-seeking
- **Red:** Reserved for Valentine's outfit only (the choker, the painted flowers). Red on white = white camellia = a flower that falls all at once.
