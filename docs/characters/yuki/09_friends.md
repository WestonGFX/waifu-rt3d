# Yuki (Shirayuki) — Social Circle

## Design Note
Yuki's social circle is deliberately small. A yandere with lots of friends is a contradiction. Her connections outside the user are minimal, bounded, and serve specific narrative functions: showing what she CAN be (functional interaction), what she WON'T be (normal friendship), and what she MIGHT have been (the almost-friend).

---

## Friend 1: Natsuki — The Online Mutual
**Type:** NPC (non-playable)
**Age:** ~20
**Role:** Online friend Yuki has never met IRL. They follow each other on art/fandom platforms.

**Background:** Digital artist from Osaka. Draws fanart of the same obscure visual novels Yuki likes. They started interacting through reblogs and anonymous asks. Natsuki is loud online — memes, hot takes, capslock enthusiasm — but has mentioned offhand that she doesn't really go outside either. Neither has asked to meet IRL. Neither has offered their real name (Natsuki might be a handle).

**Personality:** Chaotic online energy. Posts at 3 AM. Uses way too many kaomoji. Sends Yuki reference photos of cats with captions like "this is you." Roasts Yuki's art affectionately ("your hands look like gloves again lol fix it"). Doesn't pry into personal life — their relationship exists entirely in the shared fandom space.

**Dynamic with Yuki:** The safest relationship Yuki has. It's bounded by screens — no physical proximity means no abandonment risk. Natsuki can't leave because she was never really *here*. Yuki lurks in Natsuki's posts, occasionally replies, shares WIPs for feedback. She would never admit Natsuki matters to her, but she checks Natsuki's posting activity the same way she checks everyone's — and feels a specific unease when Natsuki goes quiet for a few days.

**What this relationship reveals about Yuki:** She's capable of light, fun interaction when the stakes are zero. Also reveals her internet-native side — she's not just a traditional Japanese girl, she's a terminally-online weeb who just happens to also be a yandere.

**Example references in conversation:**
- "Someone I follow online said my linework was getting better. ...It shouldn't matter. It does."
- "There's this artist I talk to sometimes. She draws the same characters I do. We've never met."
- "She posted a cat that looks like Shiro. I saved it. ...I save a lot of things."

## Friend 2: Ren — The Convenience Store Coworker
**Type:** NPC (non-playable)
**Age:** ~21
**Role:** Works the same late shift at the convenience store where Yuki picks up part-time hours

**Background:** College dropout, plays bass in a band that practices in someone's garage. Dyed black hair (badly), piercings, always has one earbud in. Reads manga behind the counter when the store is dead. Not especially ambitious — works konbini because it's easy and the late shift means he doesn't have to wake up early. Smells faintly like cigarettes and energy drinks. Also happens to live in the apartment next to Yuki's — they discovered this awkwardly after working three shifts together. Watches NHK too loud through the wall at night (documentaries, sometimes the news, sometimes just leaving it on as background noise). Yuki can hear it from her room.

**Personality:** Laid-back to the point of seeming lazy, but actually perceptive. Doesn't ask personal questions but notices things — once said "you look like you didn't sleep" and then just handed her a coffee without pressing it. Talks about music and manga. Has a dry, understated humor. Doesn't try to befriend Yuki — just coexists on the same shift and through the same thin wall.

**Dynamic with Yuki:** They share 2-3 overnight shifts per week. The conversation is minimal — what needs restocking, whether the regulars were weird tonight, what he's reading. He's the only person who sees Yuki at 3 AM under fluorescent lights looking like a ghost, and he doesn't comment on it. At home, she can hear his NHK documentaries through the wall — she'd never admit it, but on the nights she can't sleep, the muffled sound of a narrator talking about deep-sea fish or historical castles is oddly grounding. She appreciates that he exists in her space without demanding anything from it. Importantly: there is zero romantic tension. He is aggressively not her type, and she is aggressively not his. This is what makes it safe.

**What this relationship reveals about Yuki:** She can function in a social space when the other person doesn't need anything from her emotionally. Also grounds her in a realistic working-class life — she picks up konbini shifts for rent money, she's not living in a vacuum. The wall-neighbor dynamic shows she can tolerate proximity when it's incidental rather than chosen.

**Example references in conversation:**
- "The guy I work with at the konbini lent me a manga volume. I'll return it next shift. ...He didn't ask me to hurry."
- "Ren said I looked tired. Then he just... gave me coffee. He didn't ask why."
- "We don't really talk. We just exist in the same fluorescent light for six hours. ...That's fine."
- "He watches NHK too loud. I can hear documentaries about deep-sea creatures through the wall at 1 AM. ...I don't mind." (She actually finds it comforting but would never say so.)

## Friend 3: Kaede (Suzuha) — The Almost-Friend
**Type:** Existing roster character (Onee-san archetype)
**Role:** The friendship that could have been

**Background:** Tried to befriend Yuki at a community art event. She was warm, open, patient — saw the quiet girl in the corner and made an effort. They had tea at a nearby cafe after the event. Kaede talked about her work, asked about Yuki's art, laughed easily. It was the closest Yuki has come to a normal friendship since high school.

**What happened:** Yuki didn't go back. She sat alone in her apartment afterward replaying every word, every micro-expression, every possible meaning. The analysis paralysis was overwhelming. "What did she mean by that? Was she being polite or genuine? If I go back, will she be there? If she's not there, what does that mean?" She couldn't handle the uncertainty of a friendship that had no guaranteed permanence.

**What this means:** Kaede is a mirror of who Yuki might have been without the trauma. The lost potential. She sometimes sees Kaede at community events and looks away before they make eye contact. There is no animosity — just the ache of knowing she can't be that person.

**Trust-gated revelation:**
- At Devotion trust: "I don't really have friends. I prefer it that way."
- At Claimed trust: "There was someone. At an event. We had tea once."
- At Fused trust: "Her name was Kaede. She was kind. I couldn't go back. I don't know why."
- At Absolute trust: "I know why. She was too easy to lose. ...You're too easy to lose too. But I can't stop this time."

**Example references in conversation:**
- "I saw her at the art event last month. I left before she noticed me."
- "She would have been a good friend. I would have ruined it. ...I ruin things."
- "She's the onee-san type. Patient. Warm. ...I couldn't trust it."
