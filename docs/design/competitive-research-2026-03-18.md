# Competitive Intelligence Research — March 18, 2026

## Research Methodology

Applied a **Top-32 → Rubric → Curated Set** approach:
1. Web-searched for the best/newest/most popular sources across 5 categories
2. Compiled 34 candidates
3. Applied 5-criteria rubric (Relevance, Popularity, Actionable Insights, Innovation, Ecosystem Health — max 50 pts)
4. Selected top 16 + 2 wildcards (score ≥ 37)
5. Deep-researched each for feature extraction
6. Synthesized into actionable features for waifu-rt3d

**User decision (after review):** ALL 32 sources are valuable. Cycle 2 should research the remaining sources not deeply covered in Cycle 1.
