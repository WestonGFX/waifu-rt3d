## Regulatory & Industry Context (2025-2026)

Key developments that affect our content gating design:

1. **OpenAI Adult Mode** (early 2026): Age verification via Persona, age prediction AI, separate teen/adult policies
2. **California SB 243** (Jan 1, 2026): AI chatbots must disclose non-human status, protocols for suicide/self-harm content, minors protected
3. **EU AI Act**: Users must know they're talking to AI, manipulative systems targeting children prohibited
4. **Industry trend**: Stricter age gates + explicit content tiers (not binary on/off) becoming standard

**Implications for waifu-rt3d**:
- Age verification gate is now industry-standard (not optional)
- Content tiers (general/edgy/mature/explicit) are better than binary NSFW toggle
- Cloud provider ceiling enforcement is legally prudent
- Local-only processing is our strongest privacy differentiator
