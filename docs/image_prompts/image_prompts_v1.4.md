# Image Generation Prompts — v1.4 (Ultra-Detail Showcase)

## Meta
- Source: Character Bible v1.2 (12 characters)
- Style: 2D Anime / Cel-Shaded / Pixel Art (NO 3D/Realism)
- Updated: Mar 2, 2026
- Detail Level: Maximum (5-10 sentences per prompt)
- Total Prompts: 240 (12 characters × 20 categories)

## Global Style Lock

**Append to EVERY prompt:**
> 2D anime illustration, cel-shaded coloring, clean lineart, vibrant palette, no 3D rendering, no photorealism, no AI artifacts, no uncanny valley, studio-quality anime production values. No text, watermarks, or signatures visible in the image.

## Global Negative Prompt

**Include as negative prompt for EVERY generation:**
> 3D render, photorealistic, CGI, Unreal Engine, blender, daz3d, hyperrealistic, photograph, real person, extra fingers, extra limbs, mutated hands, bad anatomy, deformed face, cross-eyed, blurry, low quality, jpeg artifacts, watermark, signature, text, logo, username, words, letters, numbers, UI elements, speech bubbles, thought bubbles, subtitles, credits

## Framing & Anatomy Rules

1. **Hands:** All visible hands must have exactly five fingers per hand. Fingers should be naturally proportioned and posed. Avoid clenched fists when possible — open or relaxed hands reproduce better.
2. **Full-Body shots (categories 3, 4, 16, 17):** Both feet must be fully visible in the frame. The character should be framed with at least 10% headroom and 10% floor space.
3. **Portraits and Close-Ups:** Crop at mid-chest or above. Never crop through the chin, forehead, or ears.
4. **Eyes:** Consistent iris color per character. Heterochromia characters (Luna) must have correct L/R assignment in every image.
5. **Hair:** Maintain character-specific hair color, length, and style. Accessories (clips, pins, ribbons) must be present unless the prompt explicitly removes them.
6. **Skin tone:** Maintain consistent skin tone per character across all prompts (Pale, Fair, Sunkissed, Tanned as specified).
7. **Age consistency:** Characters must look their specified age. No aging up or down.
